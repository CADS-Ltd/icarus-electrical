Icarus Electric: website concepts

Three directions for Kevin's new website. Each uses the same brand (the red sun and lightning bolt, "Test · Install · Inspect") and follows the structure of the sites Kevin likes, eliteelectrical.co.uk and efixx.co.uk. They're kept simple, with subtle animation, clear services and a strong call to action on every page.

Concept 1

Eclipse

Closest to Elite Electrical

Dark and premium, like the black panels on the van. A glowing sun with a bolt that draws itself in, a featured project, case-study cards and expandable service pillars.

In every concept

  • Services, recent work, about Kevin and the Icarus story, reviews and an enquiry form.
  • NICEIC Approved Contractor shown up front, with "Test · Install · Inspect" and the Thomas Edison quote from the van.
  • Built for Google: page titles and descriptions, local business schema and service-led headings.
  • Works on phones, and the animation turns off for anyone who has reduced motion switched on.
