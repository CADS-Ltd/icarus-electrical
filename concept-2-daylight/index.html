FAQs

Good questions

Are you NICEIC approved?
Yes. Icarus Electric is an NICEIC Approved Contractor, so notifiable work is registered and certified to current regulations.
Do you work on homes and businesses?
Both. Most of our work is in homes, but we also cover shops, offices, restaurants, schools and other commercial spaces.
How often should my electrics be tested?
As a guide, owner-occupied homes every ten years, rented homes every five years (or at each change of tenancy), and businesses as advised on their last report.
Will you leave my home clean and tidy?
Always. Floors and furniture are protected while we work, and clean, tidy finishes come as standard.
