Talk about your job
01Rewires & new installations
Full and partial rewires, extensions and new builds wired from scratch. Routed neatly, made good and fully certified.

  • Extensions & loft conversions
  • New-build first & second fix

Replace an old fuse box with a modern consumer unit, with RCBO and surge protection to current regulations.

Fit-outs and maintenance for offices, shops, restaurants and schools. Experience across banks, schools and hospitality.

About Icarus

Inspired by legends. Going the extra mile.

Icarus Electric was inspired by the legend of Icarus, with the ethos of going beyond what's possible. Nothing is a problem, just another challenge to overcome.

Run by Kevin Peck, with ten years' experience building houses from scratch and working in banks, schools, restaurants and even Berlin Zoo. Mainly working in homes, Kevin prides himself on thorough testing and giving people something they didn't even know was possible.
